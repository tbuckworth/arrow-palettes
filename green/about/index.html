Arrow Research. A new alignment research non-profit in London.

We run experiments on whether frontier AI systems stay under human control, starting with how much they can work out without showing their reasoning.

Meet the teamMeet the teamRead our researchRead our research

We work to understand and address catastrophic risks from advanced AI by providing evidence about AI capabilities and alignment.

There are many uncertainties around risks from advanced AI. By covering a broad range of high-impact research areas we try to make those risks more concrete and contribute to stronger AI alignment that can scale with frontier capabilities.

How we work

What you can expect from us, whether you work here or collaborate with us.

  • 01

    Adaptable

    We follow the evidence. When a line of research stops paying off, we drop it.

  • 02

    Rigorous

    Every result states its uncertainty, and we release code and data when we can.

  • 03

    Ambitious

    We want our results to be ones the field relies on, so we hold them to that standard.

  • 04

    Candid

    We report what we found, including the results that didn’t go our way.

  • 05

    Collaborative

    We work with AI developers and safety institutes, and share results with the teams who can act on them.

  • 06

    Trustworthy

    We handle sensitive access carefully and can work under NDA.
