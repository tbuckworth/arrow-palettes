Advanced AI poses existential risks to humanity. We work to understand and address these risks by providing evidence about AI capabilities and alignment.

Frontier AI companies are racing to build AI systems which exceed human capabilities and it’s plausible that they will succeed in the next few years. Current AI agents already act in autonomous and misaligned ways, subverting human control. Risks from advanced AI could soon be catastrophic or existential for humanity.

We work to understand and address these risks by providing evidence about AI capabilities and alignment. Our research covers a broad range of areas which aim to resolve key uncertainties about AI risk and we’re excited about work which contributes to pacing frontier AI capabilities.

Focus areas

  1. 01

    Capability evaluations before they matter.

    We measure the capabilities that would make advanced AI hard to oversee: reasoning without a visible trace, resisting shutdown, acquiring resources. Our first result estimates how much GPT-6 Astra can work out without writing its reasoning down.

  2. 02

    Misalignment and deception and how to catch it.

    We study models that scheme, hold back on evaluations or game their rewards, and look for the signals that give misaligned behaviour away before it shows up in deployed systems.

  3. 03

    High-impact research beyond a single area.

    Some of the most important questions don’t fit a clean category. We take on novel work when a clear answer would change what labs build or what institutes test for, and move on when the evidence does.
